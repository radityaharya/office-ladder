🎮 DEADLINE DASH

Corporate Satire Board Game · 2–6 Players · 20–120 Minutes

HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

👷 Worker

Be the first player to reach the Director rank.

🕵️ Management (Hidden)

Make sure nobody becomes Director before all Meeting & Event cards run out.

⚠️ You don't know who is Management. Ratio: 3 Workers : 1 Management.

🛠️ Setup

  1. Place the board — 4 corners are fixed
  2. Shuffle all 6 card decks separately
  3. Place the top 3 Meeting deck cards face-up
  4. Deal 1 Character card + Management Identity card to each player (keep secret)
  5. All pawns start at Receptionist
  6. Each player starts with $1,000 · Rep 0 · Energy 5 · 1 MOVE token

😵 Burnout: If Energy = 0 → skip your turn, Energy automatically refills to full.  |  🏢 CEO's Office: Attempt a promotion any time you land here.

📊 Resources & Tokens

💰 Money

Used for promotions & fines. Earned from salary (passing Receptionist) & cards.

🌟 Reputation

Minimum requirement for promotions. Does not decrease when you promote.

⚡ Energy

Work tile = -1 Energy. Refill to FULL at Pantry / Lunch Break / Employee Lounge / Smoking Area.

🔢 Work Counter

Tracks how many times you've landed on Work tiles. Every 5× = +1 Rep automatically (cumulative).

4 Tokens: 🟦 MOVE (+1 space) 🟩 MOMENTUM (step back / reroll) 🟥 REP (+1 Rep) 💰 MONEY (+$200)

🗺️ Tile Types

💼 Work 📋 Meeting 🎉 Event 💬 Networking 🍽️ Energy Restore ⚙️ Special 🏢 CEO's Office 😵 Burnout Tile

💼 Work → -1 Energy + draw 1 Work Card
📋 Meeting → Draw 1 Meeting Card  |  🎉 Event → Draw 1 Event Card
💬 Networking → Draw 1 Networking Card (read the card wording out loud!)
🍽️ Restore → Refill Energy to full  |  😵 Burnout Tile → Skip 2 turns
📚 Training → Roll 2d6: 2–6 = +1 Rep, 7–12 = +2 Rep
👔 HR → Roll 2d6: Doubles = safe, anything else = -1 Rep
💸 Finance → Pay $300  |  📣 Marketing → +1 Rep
⭐ CEO's Fav → +2 Rep  |  🏆 Best Employee → +$500 +3 Rep

4 Corners: 🟢 Receptionist (START · collect salary)  🔵 Board Meeting (draw global card)  🔴 Audit (trapped: roll true doubles both dice same number or pay $500)  🟡 Annual Event (draw global card)

🃏 Card System

💼 Work Cards (50) — drawn on Work tiles
📋 Meeting Cards (50) — drawn on Meeting tiles ⏱ TIMER
🎉 Event Cards (50) — drawn on Event tiles ⏱ TIMER
💬 Networking Cards (47) — Coffee / Gossip / Seminar
🏛️ Board Meeting Cards (25) — Board Meeting corner
🎊 Annual Event Cards (25) — Annual Event corner
⏱ When the Meeting + Event decks are both empty → Management wins. All other decks reshuffle when empty.

🕵️ Management Abilities (Secret)

🔀 Shuffle Deck — 3× per Management player

Reshuffle any one deck of your choice. Everyone sees that a shuffle happened, but nobody knows who did it. Identity stays hidden.

🚫 Block Promotion — 1× per Management player

Cancel any player's promotion attempt, 100% effective. But your identity as Management is immediately revealed! Use it at the right moment.

After a Block: that Management player can still play normally and shuffle. Other players now know who they are — but a second Management player may still be hidden.

🏆 How to Win

👑
Individual Win (Worker or Management)

The first player to reach Director wins immediately. The game ends right then.

🕵️
Management Wins (Collectively)

If the Meeting + Event decks run out before anyone becomes Director → all Management players win together (even though they never knew each other).

6 Characters: The Workaholic · Social Butterfly · Sales Star · Tech Genius · Office Politician · Lucky Employee
Each has a Passive (always active) and an Active skill (has a cooldown).
💡 Key strategy: Watch who avoids attacking the leading player. Who passes through Meeting tiles when the deck looks favorable? That might be Management.
